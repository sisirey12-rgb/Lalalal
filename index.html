Welcome to RATAN

Good food, good mood.

RATAN is a family spot with a bar in Dando, Caranzalem. Come hungry for Chinese favourites: rice and noodles, crispy starters, momos and soups. Stay for Indian curries, thalis and seafood, with beers, spirits and wine at the bar. Bring the whole family, pull up a table and share a little of everything.

Can't make it in? We deliver free within 5 km. Order on WhatsApp and we will take it from there.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you deliver?

Yes, free home delivery within 5 km. Please allow 20–25 minutes for hot food.

Can I cancel an order?

Orders cannot be cancelled once placed, so please double-check your items before sending.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged directly with the restaurant when we confirm your order.

Are prices final?

Prices are in ₹ and exclude VAT and GST, which are charged extra as applicable. A few items are priced by size and we confirm those when we reply.

Can I bring outside food or drinks?

Outside food and liquor are not allowed. Smoking is not permitted inside.

Is a table booking instant?

No. Your request goes to our WhatsApp and your table is confirmed once we reply.

Parties & groups

Celebrating with a crowd?

Birthday, family get-together or an office lunch? Send us the date and the headcount and we will get back to you on WhatsApp.
